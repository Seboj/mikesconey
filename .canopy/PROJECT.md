# Mike's Coney Island

## What This Is

A website and future mobile app for Mike's Coney Island, a restaurant located in Holly, Michigan. The website will showcase the restaurant's brand, display the menu, and provide essential information for customers. A future phase will include an app with inventory management capabilities.

## Core Value

Customers can find Mike's Coney Island online, view the full menu, and get the information they need to visit or order — presented in a way that reflects the restaurant's personality and charm.
